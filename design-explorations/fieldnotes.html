Room to think

An essay about keeping our ideas open.

Philosophy

I want to leave a little space between an idea and my attachment to it. A thought can matter to me without becoming the last word.

A useful kind of doubt

Certainty makes us careless. [1] That is the sentence I wrote first. On reflection, it asks more of the evidence than I have shown.

Perhaps the more interesting question is what helps us notice when an explanation has stopped working.

Keep this question nearby: what would make me change my mind?
