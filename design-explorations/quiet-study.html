Room to think

On leaving a little space between an idea and a certainty.

Paragraph│BILinkQuote

I want a place to think in public without confusing confidence with understanding. A useful essay should leave room for its own revision.

An unfamiliar perspective can help us notice the assumptions in our own argument. [1] But what would count as evidence, and what would merely repeat something I already believe?

A more useful question

Perhaps I should begin by asking what might change my mind. That question makes the next sentence more interesting to write.

Leave the question open for a moment.
